# FLUX Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

**The short version:** FLUX works offline and has no ads and no analytics. You can play as a **Guest** (everything stays on your phone) or sign in with **Google** so your progress is saved to your account and follows you to other phones.

## Guest mode
Nothing leaves your phone. If you uninstall FLUX or change phones, your progress is lost.

## Google sign-in, cloud save and leaderboards
If you choose "Continue with Google", Firebase receives your Google account's name, email address and unique account ID. Your game progress (levels, stars, badges, streak, stats, settings, player name and avatar) is stored privately in Cloud Firestore under your account ID, so it can be restored on another phone. It is used only for account progress and is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for ads or analytics.

Global and Daily leaderboard entries are available only to signed-in Google users. Entries contain a random pseudonymous player key and puzzle scores; they do not contain your email, Firebase account ID, Google name or photo. "Delete my cloud data" removes your private progress and the private mapping between your account and leaderboard key. Previously submitted pseudonymous leaderboard scores remain in rankings. Signing out keeps your cloud progress and scores.

If you switch directly from one Google account to another, FLUX keeps the previous account's progress in a separate local recovery copy so it is never merged into the newly selected account. Sign back into the original account to restore and sync that copy. It remains on this phone until that account syncs successfully, its cloud data is deleted, or FLUX is uninstalled.

## What is stored on your phone
Your player name and avatar colour, your progress (levels, stars, badges, streaks, stats), your settings, and an unfinished puzzle so you can resume it. This data lives only in the app storage of your phone. Uninstalling FLUX deletes it. (If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may back this data up to your own account and restore it on a new phone; we do not receive it.)

## Notifications
If you turn on reminders, FLUX asks Android for permission and schedules notifications on your phone: a daily puzzle reminder, a streak reminder, and a nudge if you have been away. They are created locally by the app, not sent by a server. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone settings at any time.

## Crash information
If the app hits an error, a short note is kept on your phone. It is only shared if you choose "Share error report".

## Children
FLUX has no ads or analytics and is suitable for all ages. Google account details are used for sign-in and private cloud progress. Please do not type a real full name as a player nickname if you are under 13; any nickname works.

## Deleting your data
Settings > Reset progress clears your progress (and your cloud save if you are signed in). Settings > Account > Delete my cloud data permanently removes cloud progress and the private leaderboard-key mapping; pseudonymous scores remain in rankings. Uninstalling the app removes local data. You can also request deletion by email.

# FLUX Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

**The short version:** FLUX works offline and has no ads and no analytics. You can play as a **Guest** (everything stays on your phone) or sign in with **Google** so your progress is saved to your account and follows you to other phones.

## Guest mode
Nothing leaves your phone. If you uninstall FLUX or change phones, your progress is lost.

## Google sign-in and cloud save
If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your Google account's name, email address and unique account ID from Google Sign-In. Your game progress (levels, stars, badges, streak, stats, settings, player name and avatar) is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) under your account ID, so it can be restored on any phone you sign in on. It is used only to provide this save feature. It is not sold, shared with advertisers or used for ads or analytics. Only you can read your save. You can delete it at any time in Settings > Account > "Delete my cloud data", or by emailing the address below. Signing out keeps your cloud save; deleting removes it.

## What is stored on your phone
Your player name and avatar colour, your progress (levels, stars, badges, streaks, stats), your settings, and an unfinished puzzle so you can resume it. This data lives only in the app storage of your phone. Uninstalling FLUX deletes it. (If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may back this data up to your own account and restore it on a new phone; we do not receive it.)

## Notifications
If you turn on reminders, FLUX asks Android for permission and schedules notifications on your phone: a daily puzzle reminder, a streak reminder, and a nudge if you have been away. They are created locally by the app, not sent by a server. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone settings at any time.

## Crash information
If the app hits an error, a short note is kept on your phone. It is only shared if you choose "Share error report".

## Children
FLUX collects no personal information and is suitable for all ages. Please do not type a real full name if you are under 13; any nickname works.

## Deleting your data
Settings > Reset progress clears your progress (and your cloud save if you are signed in). Settings > Account > Delete my cloud data permanently removes your cloud save. Uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone. You can also request deletion by email.

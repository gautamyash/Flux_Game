Design system and screens · v1.0 · Android first, React Native ready

FLUX

Connect. Adapt. Solve.

A calm, precise path puzzle. One finger draws one continuous line through numbered nodes while the board changes under it. Everything on these artboards is built from the same tokens, glyphs and board components an engineer will implement.

01

The board is the hero

Board fills 92% of screen width, the largest square a portrait phone allows (about 42% of its height). In landscape it takes the full height. HUD stays at the edges and never overlaps it.

02

One path, one accent

Flux Teal is the only brand hue. Chosen because it reads as calm and technical, sits far from LinkedIn blue, and keeps 3:1 against warm paper and warm black.

03

Shape before colour

Every special cell has its own glyph and pattern. Tint is a hint, never the signal. Hatch means wall, chevron means direction, ring means portal.

04

Explain, don't punish

Invalid moves retract one step and say why in one calm line. Failure is a pause with an undo, not a penalty screen.

05

Motion carries meaning

Short, eased, purposeful: 80 to 260ms for play, up to 900ms for a solve. Reduced motion swaps every move for a fade.

06

Built from tokens

4pt grid, a short radius scale, rounded rects, circles and SVG glyphs only. Nothing here needs more than SVG, Skia or Reanimated.

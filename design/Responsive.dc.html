Everything outside the board is fixed in dp. The board zone is the flexible row and always wins the leftover space.

Sizing logic

availableWidth = screenW − 2 × margin  (margin 16 phone, 24 tablet)
availableHeight = screenH − safeTop − topBar 56 − HUD 60
                 − tracker 48 − feedback 56 − actionBar 100 − inset
boardSide = min(availableWidth, availableHeight, 760)
cellSize  = floor(boardSide / gridSize)   (4 … 8, 9 and 10 in Endless)
if cellSize < 40dp → cellSize = 40, board pans and pinch-zooms inside its frame
  • The board is centred in its zone; cell size is always an integer so strokes stay crisp.
  • Safe areas: top 44 (up to 48 with a cutout), bottom 24 for the gesture bar (16 minimum), 24 each side in landscape.
  • Tablet: board capped at 760dp and centred; optional side panel for stats at 340dp.
  • Landscape: board on the left at full height, HUD, tracker, feedback and actions stacked on the right.
  • Touch targets stay 48dp or more at every size, including a 40dp cell with its 3% gap.

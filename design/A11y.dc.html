Colour-blind simulation

Protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia and grayscale proofs of every special cell are on the Special cell legend artboard. Result: no two families share a silhouette; portal pairs are told apart by dot, triangle and square; direction is told by chevron orientation; error state adds an outline, never only a tint.

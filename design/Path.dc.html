Touch behaviour

  • Hit target is the whole cell, not the glyph.
  • Hysteresis: the path enters the next cell once the finger is 35% past the shared edge; diagonals resolve to the dominant axis.
  • Path always snaps to cell centres; never freehand.
  • Dragging back onto the previous cell retracts one step.
  • Finger offset: when a cell is under 56dp the head cursor is drawn 0.8 × cell above the touch point so the finger never hides it.
  • Lifting the finger keeps the path. A tap on the head or any committed cell resumes from there.
